About Us

Dunedin's Most Experienced Plumbing Team

Barry Dell Plumbing has been serving Dunedin and Otago for over 50 years. Under the ownership of Barry and Liz Carmichael since 2001, we've grown to a team of 25 dedicated professionals — all committed to quality workmanship, clear communication, and leaving every job site clean.

Whether it's a dripping tap, a full bathroom renovation, underfloor heating, gas fitting, or commercial drainage — we have the people, the equipment, and the experience to handle it properly.

What We Do

Full-Service Plumbing Solutions

From residential maintenance to large commercial projects, our licensed team covers every aspect of plumbing, drainage, roofing, and gas fitting across Dunedin and Otago.

Plumbing & Repairs

General maintenance, leak repairs, tapware, hot water cylinders, and everything in between. Fast, reliable, done right.

Bathroom Renovations

Complete bathroom and laundry fitouts. All plumbing, drainage, and fittings — built to last and finished to a high standard.

Kitchen Plumbing

New kitchen installs, sink replacements, dishwasher connections, and water filtration. Clean, tidy workmanship every time.

Underfloor Heating

Hydronic underfloor heating systems designed for Dunedin's climate. Energy-efficient, comfortable, and properly installed.

Drainage

Stormwater, wastewater, blocked drains, CCTV inspection, and new drainage systems for residential and commercial properties.

Shower & Wet Areas

New shower enclosures, tile-over systems, and full wet-area installations. Waterproofed and built to code.

Gas Fitting

Natural gas and LPG connections, appliance installations, gas line testing, and compliance certifications by fully licensed gasfitters.

Roofing & Guttering

Metal roofing, roof repairs, guttering installation, gutter cleaning, and ventilation systems. Roofing Association NZ members.
